# XView icons

All icons in this directory are original drawings made for XView. None are
copied or traced from TradingView or any other product. They share one style:
28x28 viewBox, 1.5 stroke, round caps and joins, `currentColor`, and fills only
on small dots.

They are licensed with the rest of XView under GPL-3.0-or-later.
